# Changelog

## 1.0.0 – 2026-11-04

First public release, derived from a private card (`wp-energie-card` 0.1.5) used on a
Buderus WLW186i with EMS-ESP.

- Overview, monthly history and yearly efficiency views
- Configurable entity IDs with EMS-ESP defaults; automatic start month
- English and German UI; numbers and months follow the Home Assistant locale
- Per-user month cache in `frontend/user_data`
